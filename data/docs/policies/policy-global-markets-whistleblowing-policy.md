# Global Markets Whistleblowing Policy

Version 5.4. Effective October 1, 2025. Policy owner: Head Of Compliance.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation.

## Scope

Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business.

## Definitions

First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff. Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it.

## Policy Requirements

1. Reports made through the confidential hotline are triaged by the Group Treasurer within 6 business days. Deviations are logged and escalated to the policy owner.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Internal Audit performs independent assurance and reports material findings to the Audit Committee of the Board. The Compliance function provides advice, monitoring and periodic testing of the controls described here.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Compliance performs thematic reviews on a risk-based schedule and shares the results with business management and Internal Audit. Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately.

## Exceptions

All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner. Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
