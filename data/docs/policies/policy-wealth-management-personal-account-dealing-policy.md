# Wealth Management Personal Account Dealing Policy

Version 3.1. Effective April 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Risk Officer.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions.

## Scope

It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum.

## Definitions

Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff. Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it.

## Policy Requirements

1. Staff must hold any personally purchased security for a minimum of 30 calendar days before selling it. Deviations are logged and escalated to the policy owner.

2. Pre-clearance approvals expire at the end of the trading day after 9 business day(s) if the order is not executed. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time. The Compliance function provides advice, monitoring and periodic testing of the controls described here.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Compliance performs thematic reviews on a risk-based schedule and shares the results with business management and Internal Audit. Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected. Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
