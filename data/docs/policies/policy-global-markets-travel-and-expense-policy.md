# Global Markets Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3.2. Effective April 1, 2025. Policy owner: Divisional Chief Operating Officer.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation.

## Scope

The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum.

## Definitions

Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff.

## Policy Requirements

1. Employees may book business class only for flights longer than 4 hours of scheduled flying time. This requirement is monitored through automated surveillance.

2. The nightly hotel cap is USD 435,000 in tier-one cities such as London and New York. Deviations are logged and escalated to the policy owner.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter. The Compliance function provides advice, monitoring and periodic testing of the controls described here.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification. Compliance performs thematic reviews on a risk-based schedule and shares the results with business management and Internal Audit.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile. Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication.
