# Global Markets Wire Transfer Authorization Policy

Version 3.9. Effective January 1, 2025. Policy owner: Group Treasurer.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm.

## Scope

It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group.

## Definitions

Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities.

## Policy Requirements

1. Any single outgoing wire above USD 1,015,000 requires dual authorisation by two Treasury officers before release. This requirement is monitored through automated surveillance.

2. Call-back verification to a pre-registered phone number is mandatory when beneficiary bank details change within 5 days of a payment. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time. Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Trend analysis of breaches is presented to the Board Risk Committee twice a year. Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately.

## Exceptions

All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner. Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile. Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication.
