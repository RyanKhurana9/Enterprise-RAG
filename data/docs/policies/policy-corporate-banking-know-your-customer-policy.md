# Corporate Banking Know Your Customer Policy

Version 6.8. Effective January 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer.

## Purpose

It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation.

## Scope

The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum.

## Definitions

Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities.

## Policy Requirements

1. High-risk clients undergo a full periodic due-diligence refresh every 9 months, medium-risk clients every 20 months. This requirement is monitored through automated surveillance.

2. Beneficial owners holding 120 percent or more of a legal entity must be identified and verified. This requirement is monitored through automated surveillance.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter. Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification. Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
