# Global Markets Conflicts of Interest Policy

Version 2.4. Effective April 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm.

## Scope

The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum.

## Definitions

Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. Second line of defence refers to the independent Risk and Compliance functions that set standards and challenge the first line.

## Policy Requirements

1. Outside business activities must be disclosed and approved within 21 days of the employee accepting the role. This requirement is monitored through automated surveillance.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter. Internal Audit performs independent assurance and reports material findings to the Audit Committee of the Board.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately. Trend analysis of breaches is presented to the Board Risk Committee twice a year.

## Exceptions

All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner. Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months.

## Breaches

Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected. Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
