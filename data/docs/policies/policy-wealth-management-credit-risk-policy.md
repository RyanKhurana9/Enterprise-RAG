# Wealth Management Credit Risk Policy

Version 1.0. Effective April 1, 2025. Policy owner: Group Treasurer.

## Purpose

It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm.

## Scope

It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum.

## Definitions

Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Second line of defence refers to the independent Risk and Compliance functions that set standards and challenge the first line.

## Policy Requirements

1. Single-name exposure to any non-bank counterparty is capped at 10 percent of Tier 1 capital. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

2. Watch-list credits are reviewed by the Credit Committee every 2 weeks. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Internal Audit performs independent assurance and reports material findings to the Audit Committee of the Board. Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately. Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum.

## Exceptions

All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner. Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
