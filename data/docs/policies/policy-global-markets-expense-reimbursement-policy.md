# Global Markets Expense Reimbursement Policy

Version 3.6. Effective October 1, 2025. Policy owner: Head Of Compliance.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm.

## Scope

Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group.

## Definitions

Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies.

## Policy Requirements

1. Expense claims must be submitted within 90 days of the expense being incurred, with itemised receipts above USD 20. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Internal Audit performs independent assurance and reports material findings to the Audit Committee of the Board. The Compliance function provides advice, monitoring and periodic testing of the controls described here.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification. Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
