# Wealth Management Conflicts of Interest Policy

Version 3.2. Effective January 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Risk Officer.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm.

## Scope

Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business.

## Definitions

Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies.

## Policy Requirements

1. Outside business activities must be disclosed and approved within 7 days of the employee accepting the role. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter. Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Compliance performs thematic reviews on a risk-based schedule and shares the results with business management and Internal Audit. Trend analysis of breaches is presented to the Board Risk Committee twice a year.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile. Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication.
