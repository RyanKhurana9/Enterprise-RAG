# Global Markets Remote Working Policy

Version 1.3. Effective October 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer.

## Purpose

It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm.

## Scope

It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group.

## Definitions

First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. Second line of defence refers to the independent Risk and Compliance functions that set standards and challenge the first line.

## Policy Requirements

1. Employees may work remotely up to 8 days per week subject to manager approval. Deviations are logged and escalated to the policy owner.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The Compliance function provides advice, monitoring and periodic testing of the controls described here. Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification. Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum.

## Exceptions

All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner. Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months.

## Breaches

Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected. Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
