# Wealth Management Cybersecurity Incident Response Policy

Version 6.6. Effective July 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions.

## Scope

It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum.

## Definitions

Second line of defence refers to the independent Risk and Compliance functions that set standards and challenge the first line. Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies.

## Policy Requirements

1. Severity-one incidents must be escalated to the Chief Financial Officer within 60 minutes of confirmation. Controls are tested quarterly by the first line of defence.

2. A post-incident review must be completed within 14 days of closing a severity-one or severity-two incident. Controls are tested quarterly by the first line of defence.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter. Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum. Trend analysis of breaches is presented to the Board Risk Committee twice a year.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
