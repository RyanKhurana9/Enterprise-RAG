# Global Markets Business Continuity Policy

Version 1.2. Effective July 1, 2025. Policy owner: Divisional Chief Operating Officer.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions.

## Scope

Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business.

## Definitions

Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities.

## Policy Requirements

1. Critical trading applications have a recovery time objective of 90 minutes and a recovery point objective of zero data loss. Deviations are logged and escalated to the policy owner.

2. Full failover tests to the secondary data centre are performed 9 times per year. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time. Internal Audit performs independent assurance and reports material findings to the Audit Committee of the Board.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification. Compliance performs thematic reviews on a risk-based schedule and shares the results with business management and Internal Audit.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile. Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication.
