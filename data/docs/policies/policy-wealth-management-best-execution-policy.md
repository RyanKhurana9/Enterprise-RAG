# Wealth Management Best Execution Policy

Version 3.4. Effective October 1, 2025. Policy owner: Group Treasurer.

## Purpose

It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm.

## Scope

Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business.

## Definitions

Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies.

## Policy Requirements

1. Execution quality is reviewed by the Best Execution Committee every 8 weeks using transaction cost analysis. Deviations are logged and escalated to the policy owner.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The Compliance function provides advice, monitoring and periodic testing of the controls described here. Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Trend analysis of breaches is presented to the Board Risk Committee twice a year. Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected. Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile. Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication.
