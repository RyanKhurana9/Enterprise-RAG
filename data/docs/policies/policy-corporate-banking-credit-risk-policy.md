# Corporate Banking Credit Risk Policy

Version 1.3. Effective January 1, 2025. Policy owner: Divisional Chief Operating Officer.

## Purpose

It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation.

## Scope

It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum.

## Definitions

Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities.

## Policy Requirements

1. Single-name exposure to any non-bank counterparty is capped at 75 percent of Tier 1 capital. Evidence of compliance must be retained for audit.

2. Watch-list credits are reviewed by the Credit Committee every 5 weeks. Controls are tested quarterly by the first line of defence.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter. Internal Audit performs independent assurance and reports material findings to the Audit Committee of the Board.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum. Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification.

## Exceptions

All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner. Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months.

## Breaches

Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected. Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
