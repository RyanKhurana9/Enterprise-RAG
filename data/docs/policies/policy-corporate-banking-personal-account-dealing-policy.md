# Corporate Banking Personal Account Dealing Policy

Version 2.9. Effective October 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions.

## Scope

The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business.

## Definitions

Employee means any person engaged by the firm under a contract of employment, including fixed-term and part-time staff. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities.

## Policy Requirements

1. Staff must hold any personally purchased security for a minimum of 10 calendar days before selling it. Controls are tested quarterly by the first line of defence.

2. Pre-clearance approvals expire at the end of the trading day after 8 business day(s) if the order is not executed. This requirement is monitored through automated surveillance.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The Compliance function provides advice, monitoring and periodic testing of the controls described here. Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately. Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication. The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile.
