# Corporate Banking Client Complaints Handling Policy

Version 6.7. Effective April 1, 2025. Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer.

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions.

## Scope

Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group.

## Definitions

First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies. Policy owner means the senior manager accountable for maintaining this document and for approving interpretations of it.

## Policy Requirements

1. Complaints must be acknowledged in writing within 9 business days and resolved within 30 calendar days. This requirement is monitored through automated surveillance.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time. Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately. Exceptions, breaches and near misses are recorded in the operational risk system within five business days of identification.

## Exceptions

All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner. Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months.

## Breaches

Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected. Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile. Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication.
