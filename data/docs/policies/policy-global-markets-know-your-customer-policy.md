# Global Markets Know Your Customer Policy

Version 2.0. Effective July 1, 2025. Policy owner: Head Of Compliance.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to describe the controls, responsibilities and escalation paths that protect clients, employees and the firm. This policy sets out the minimum standards that apply across the division and supports the firm's obligations under applicable law and regulation. It is designed to be proportionate to the risks of the business while remaining practical for front-office and support functions.

## Scope

It covers activity conducted on firm premises, remotely, and while travelling on firm business. Where local regulation is stricter than this policy, the local requirement prevails and must be documented in a country addendum. The policy applies to all permanent employees, contractors and consultants, including staff seconded from other legal entities of the group.

## Definitions

Second line of defence refers to the independent Risk and Compliance functions that set standards and challenge the first line. First line of defence refers to the business units that own and manage risk in their day-to-day activities. Material change means any change that alters a limit, a threshold, an approval authority or the population to which the policy applies.

## Policy Requirements

1. High-risk clients undergo a full periodic due-diligence refresh every 4 months, medium-risk clients every 20 months. Controls are tested quarterly by the first line of defence.

2. Beneficial owners holding 75 percent or more of a legal entity must be identified and verified. Controls are tested quarterly by the first line of defence.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Line managers are accountable for ensuring their teams understand and follow the policy, and for completing attestations on time. Employees must complete the associated mandatory training module within thirty days of joining and annually thereafter.

## Monitoring and Reporting

Management information produced under this policy must be reconciled to source systems before it is presented to any governance forum. Key risk indicators linked to this policy are reported monthly to the divisional Risk Committee, with red-rated indicators escalated immediately.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, include a risk assessment and a remediation plan, and are valid for no longer than twelve months. All approved exceptions are recorded in the central exceptions register and reviewed quarterly by the policy owner.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to regulators. Employees who identify a breach should report it promptly to their manager or to Compliance; good-faith reporting is protected.

## Review

The policy owner reviews this document at least annually or following a material change in regulation, business model or risk profile. Material changes require approval by the divisional Risk Committee before publication.
