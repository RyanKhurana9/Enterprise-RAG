# IT Helpdesk FAQ - Singapore

Internal frequently asked questions for Singapore employees. Last updated September 2025.

## How do I reset my password?
Use the self-service portal; reset links expire after 90 minutes.

## What is the helpdesk response target?
Priority-one tickets receive a response within 90 minutes, twenty-four hours a day.

## Does this apply to contractors?
The full policy is published on the intranet policy library.

## Does this apply to contractors?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.
