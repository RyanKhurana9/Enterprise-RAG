# Payroll FAQ - United States

Internal frequently asked questions for United States employees. Last updated March 2025.

## When is salary paid?
Salary is paid on the 8th business day before month-end, by direct deposit.

## How do I change my bank account for payroll?
Submit the change in the HR portal at least 10 days before the next pay date.

## Where can I find the full policy?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.

## Where can I find the full policy?
Contact the regional HR Service Centre through the employee portal.
