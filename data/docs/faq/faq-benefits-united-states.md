# Benefits FAQ - United States

Internal frequently asked questions for United States employees. Last updated September 2025.

## What is the pension match?
The firm matches employee pension contributions up to 105 percent of base salary.

## How much parental leave do I get?
Primary caregivers receive 60 weeks of fully paid parental leave.

## Does this apply to contractors?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.

## Where can I find the full policy?
The full policy is published on the intranet policy library.
