# Client Onboarding FAQ - United States

Internal frequently asked questions for United States employees. Last updated June 2025.

## How long does onboarding take?
Standard institutional onboarding takes 5 to 15 business days once all documents are received.

## Which documents are required for a corporate client?
We require the certificate of incorporation, a register of directors and proof of address dated within the last 10 days.
