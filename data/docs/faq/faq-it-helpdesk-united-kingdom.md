# IT Helpdesk FAQ - United Kingdom

Internal frequently asked questions for United Kingdom employees. Last updated March 2025.

## How do I reset my password?
Use the self-service portal; reset links expire after 10 minutes.

## What is the helpdesk response target?
Priority-one tickets receive a response within 10 minutes, twenty-four hours a day.
