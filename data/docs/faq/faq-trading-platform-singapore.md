# Trading Platform FAQ - Singapore

Internal frequently asked questions for Singapore employees. Last updated March 2025.

## What are the platform's trading hours?
The electronic platform accepts orders from 4:00 to 22:00 local time on business days.

## What is the maximum order size online?
Single online orders are capped at USD 113,500; larger orders must be placed through a sales trader.
