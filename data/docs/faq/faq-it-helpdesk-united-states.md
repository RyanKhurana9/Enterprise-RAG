# IT Helpdesk FAQ - United States

Internal frequently asked questions for United States employees. Last updated March 2025.

## How do I reset my password?
Use the self-service portal; reset links expire after 15 minutes.

## What is the helpdesk response target?
Priority-one tickets receive a response within 15 minutes, twenty-four hours a day.

## Who do I contact with further questions?
Contact the regional HR Service Centre through the employee portal.

## Does this apply to contractors?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.
