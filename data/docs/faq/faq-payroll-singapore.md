# Payroll FAQ - Singapore

Internal frequently asked questions for Singapore employees. Last updated September 2025.

## When is salary paid?
Salary is paid on the 9th business day before month-end, by direct deposit.

## How do I change my bank account for payroll?
Submit the change in the HR portal at least 90 days before the next pay date.
