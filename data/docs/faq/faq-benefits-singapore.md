# Benefits FAQ - Singapore

Internal frequently asked questions for Singapore employees. Last updated September 2025.

## What is the pension match?
The firm matches employee pension contributions up to 10 percent of base salary.

## How much parental leave do I get?
Primary caregivers receive 80 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
