# Benefits FAQ - United Kingdom

Internal frequently asked questions for United Kingdom employees. Last updated June 2025.

## What is the pension match?
The firm matches employee pension contributions up to 75 percent of base salary.

## How much parental leave do I get?
Primary caregivers receive 30 weeks of fully paid parental leave.

## Does this apply to contractors?
Contact the regional HR Service Centre through the employee portal.

## Where can I find the full policy?
