# Client Onboarding FAQ - United Kingdom

Internal frequently asked questions for United Kingdom employees. Last updated March 2025.

## How long does onboarding take?
Standard institutional onboarding takes 2 to 30 business days once all documents are received.

## Which documents are required for a corporate client?
We require the certificate of incorporation, a register of directors and proof of address dated within the last 14 days.

## Does this apply to contractors?
Contact the regional HR Service Centre through the employee portal.

## Where can I find the full policy?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.
