# Trading Platform FAQ - United Kingdom

Internal frequently asked questions for United Kingdom employees. Last updated September 2025.

## What are the platform's trading hours?
The electronic platform accepts orders from 24:00 to 22:00 local time on business days.

## What is the maximum order size online?
Single online orders are capped at USD 21,500; larger orders must be placed through a sales trader.

## Does this apply to contractors?
Contact the regional HR Service Centre through the employee portal.

## Does this apply to contractors?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.
