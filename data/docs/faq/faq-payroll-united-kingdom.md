# Payroll FAQ - United Kingdom

Internal frequently asked questions for United Kingdom employees. Last updated June 2025.

## When is salary paid?
Salary is paid on the 6th business day before month-end, by direct deposit.

## How do I change my bank account for payroll?
Submit the change in the HR portal at least 5 days before the next pay date.

## Who do I contact with further questions?
The full policy is published on the intranet policy library.

## Where can I find the full policy?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.
