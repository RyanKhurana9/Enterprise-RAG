# Annual Leave FAQ - United Kingdom

Internal frequently asked questions for United Kingdom employees. Last updated September 2025.

## How many vacation days do I get?
Full-time employees receive 55 days of annual leave plus public holidays.

## Can I carry over unused leave?
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the first quarter of the next year.

## Who do I contact with further questions?
Contact the regional HR Service Centre through the employee portal.

## Where can I find the full policy?
Contractors should refer to the terms of their agency agreement.
