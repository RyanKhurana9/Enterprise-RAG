# Bryant Health - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2024)

## Item 1. Business
Bryant Health is a healthcare company operating through three reportable segments: Medical Devices,
Diagnostics and Pharmacy Services. Our principal executive offices are located in Charlotte, North Carolina. As of the end of fiscal 2024, we employed approximately 2,887 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 76 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $11,348 million, an increase of 16.7 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 27.0 percent and net income was
$1,790 million. The Medical Devices segment was our largest, contributing $5,637 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Medical Devices: $5,637 million
- Diagnostics: $3,649 million
- Pharmacy Services: $2,061 million

Results reflected cost discipline partly offset by
restructuring charges.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $1,218 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $3,865 million. Capital expenditures were $1,119 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $2.88 per share and authorised a $277 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
