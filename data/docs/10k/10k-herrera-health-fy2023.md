# Herrera Health - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Herrera Health is a healthcare company operating through three reportable segments: Medical Devices,
Diagnostics and Pharmacy Services. Our principal executive offices are located in Portland, Oregon. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 80,902 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 32 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $6,562 million, an increase of 17.0 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 14.3 percent and net income was
$741 million. The Medical Devices segment was our largest, contributing $4,141 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Medical Devices: $4,141 million
- Diagnostics: $1,388 million
- Pharmacy Services: $1,034 million

Results reflected cost discipline partly offset by
elevated wage inflation.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $5,335 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $2,524 million. Capital expenditures were $148 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $1.20 per share and authorised a $2,413 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
