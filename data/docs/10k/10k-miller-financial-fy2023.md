# Miller Financial - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Miller Financial is a financial company operating through three reportable segments: Payments,
Asset Management and Consumer Lending. Our principal executive offices are located in Phoenix, Arizona. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 66,906 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 27 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $7,871 million, an increase of 17.5 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 17.4 percent and net income was
$1,012 million. The Payments segment was our largest, contributing $4,510 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Payments: $4,510 million
- Asset Management: $2,868 million
- Consumer Lending: $493 million

Results reflected the integration of prior acquisitions partly offset by
elevated wage inflation.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $2,228 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $458 million. Capital expenditures were $561 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $0.92 per share and authorised a $2,720 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
