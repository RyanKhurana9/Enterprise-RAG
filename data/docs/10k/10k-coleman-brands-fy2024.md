# Coleman Brands - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2024)

## Item 1. Business
Coleman Brands is a consumer company operating through three reportable segments: Beverages,
Snacks and Personal Care. Our principal executive offices are located in Tampa, Florida. As of the end of fiscal 2024, we employed approximately 18,523 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 90 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $31,239 million, an increase of 15.7 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 28.1 percent and net income was
$6,711 million. The Beverages segment was our largest, contributing $21,538 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Beverages: $21,538 million
- Snacks: $8,763 million
- Personal Care: $937 million

Results reflected a favourable mix partly offset by
foreign-exchange headwinds.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $19,708 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $5,404 million. Capital expenditures were $1,914 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $2.88 per share and authorised a $1,117 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
