# Yates Energy - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Yates Energy is a energy company operating through three reportable segments: Upstream,
Refining and Renewables. Our principal executive offices are located in Seattle, Washington. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 6,915 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 103 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $33,822 million, a decrease of 5.4 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 16.7 percent and net income was
$3,961 million. The Upstream segment was our largest, contributing $20,427 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Upstream: $20,427 million
- Refining: $9,530 million
- Renewables: $3,865 million

Results reflected a favourable mix partly offset by
elevated wage inflation.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $15,446 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $6,130 million. Capital expenditures were $2,288 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $2.10 per share and authorised a $2,328 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
