# Jackson Energy - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Jackson Energy is a energy company operating through three reportable segments: Upstream,
Refining and Renewables. Our principal executive offices are located in Denver, Colorado. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 1,220 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 39 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $38,062 million, an increase of 4.5 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 10.1 percent and net income was
$2,896 million. The Upstream segment was our largest, contributing $17,524 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Upstream: $17,524 million
- Refining: $13,100 million
- Renewables: $7,438 million

Results reflected a favourable mix partly offset by
lower demand in Europe.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $20,915 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $11,385 million. Capital expenditures were $1,285 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $0.92 per share and authorised a $2,402 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
