# Baxter Industries - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Baxter Industries is a industrial company operating through three reportable segments: Aerospace Components,
Energy Systems and Aftermarket Services. Our principal executive offices are located in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 21,565 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 48 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $36,444 million, an increase of 14.3 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 27.6 percent and net income was
$7,138 million. The Aerospace Components segment was our largest, contributing $18,650 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Aerospace Components: $18,650 million
- Energy Systems: $15,330 million
- Aftermarket Services: $2,464 million

Results reflected the integration of prior acquisitions partly offset by
elevated wage inflation.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $14,741 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $10,387 million. Capital expenditures were $742 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. We did not pay a dividend during the year and prioritised debt reduction. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
