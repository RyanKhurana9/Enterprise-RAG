# Short Technologies - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Short Technologies is a software company operating through three reportable segments: Cloud Platform,
Enterprise Licences and Professional Services. Our principal executive offices are located in Raleigh, North Carolina. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 47,779 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 56 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $20,191 million, a decrease of 5.6 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 27.3 percent and net income was
$3,213 million. The Cloud Platform segment was our largest, contributing $10,063 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Cloud Platform: $10,063 million
- Enterprise Licences: $6,856 million
- Professional Services: $3,273 million

Results reflected pricing actions partly offset by
lower demand in Europe.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $13,927 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $3,628 million. Capital expenditures were $739 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. We did not pay a dividend during the year and prioritised debt reduction. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
