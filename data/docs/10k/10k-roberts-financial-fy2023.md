# Roberts Financial - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Roberts Financial is a financial company operating through three reportable segments: Payments,
Asset Management and Consumer Lending. Our principal executive offices are located in Atlanta, Georgia. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 78,430 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 65 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $24,454 million, an increase of 3.2 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 11.4 percent and net income was
$2,158 million. The Payments segment was our largest, contributing $15,369 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Payments: $15,369 million
- Asset Management: $7,226 million
- Consumer Lending: $1,859 million

Results reflected the integration of prior acquisitions partly offset by
restructuring charges.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $11,596 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $6,162 million. Capital expenditures were $1,438 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $1.64 per share and authorised a $1,387 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
