# Burns Brands - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Burns Brands is a consumer company operating through three reportable segments: Beverages,
Snacks and Personal Care. Our principal executive offices are located in Austin, Texas. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 19,155 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 93 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $40,585 million, an increase of 15.0 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 6.9 percent and net income was
$1,762 million. The Beverages segment was our largest, contributing $19,446 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Beverages: $19,446 million
- Snacks: $18,190 million
- Personal Care: $2,949 million

Results reflected cost discipline partly offset by
higher input costs.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $23,135 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $10,222 million. Capital expenditures were $1,001 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $2.10 per share and authorised a $810 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
