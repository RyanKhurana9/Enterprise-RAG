# Romero Technologies - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Romero Technologies is a software company operating through three reportable segments: Cloud Platform,
Enterprise Licences and Professional Services. Our principal executive offices are located in Phoenix, Arizona. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 81,547 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 48 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $5,789 million, a decrease of 1.2 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 19.1 percent and net income was
$834 million. The Cloud Platform segment was our largest, contributing $5,299 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Cloud Platform: $5,299 million
- Enterprise Licences: $408 million
- Professional Services: $82 million

Results reflected pricing actions partly offset by
foreign-exchange headwinds.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $4,148 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $944 million. Capital expenditures were $530 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $1.64 per share and authorised a $1,228 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
