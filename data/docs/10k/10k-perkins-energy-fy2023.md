# Perkins Energy - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Perkins Energy is a energy company operating through three reportable segments: Upstream,
Refining and Renewables. Our principal executive offices are located in Columbus, Ohio. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 66,596 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 65 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $35,435 million, a decrease of 5.2 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 25.5 percent and net income was
$5,508 million. The Upstream segment was our largest, contributing $15,929 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Upstream: $15,929 million
- Refining: $12,657 million
- Renewables: $6,849 million

Results reflected volume growth partly offset by
elevated wage inflation.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $16,586 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $5,788 million. Capital expenditures were $3,910 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. We did not pay a dividend during the year and prioritised debt reduction. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
