# Vaughn Energy - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Vaughn Energy is a energy company operating through three reportable segments: Upstream,
Refining and Renewables. Our principal executive offices are located in Columbus, Ohio. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 94,229 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 88 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $24,415 million, a decrease of 0.7 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 10.8 percent and net income was
$1,637 million. The Upstream segment was our largest, contributing $10,090 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Upstream: $10,090 million
- Refining: $8,344 million
- Renewables: $5,982 million

Results reflected volume growth partly offset by
higher input costs.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $6,536 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $1,827 million. Capital expenditures were $1,745 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. We did not pay a dividend during the year and prioritised debt reduction. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
