# Walker Energy - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2024)

## Item 1. Business
Walker Energy is a energy company operating through three reportable segments: Upstream,
Refining and Renewables. Our principal executive offices are located in Columbus, Ohio. As of the end of fiscal 2024, we employed approximately 75,028 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 92 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $46,547 million, an increase of 2.4 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 28.1 percent and net income was
$7,662 million. The Upstream segment was our largest, contributing $22,362 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Upstream: $22,362 million
- Refining: $15,036 million
- Renewables: $9,148 million

Results reflected a favourable mix partly offset by
elevated wage inflation.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $32,975 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $5,084 million. Capital expenditures were $4,033 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $2.88 per share and authorised a $1,763 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
