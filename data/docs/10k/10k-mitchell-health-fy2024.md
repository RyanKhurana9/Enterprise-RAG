# Mitchell Health - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2024)

## Item 1. Business
Mitchell Health is a healthcare company operating through three reportable segments: Medical Devices,
Diagnostics and Pharmacy Services. Our principal executive offices are located in Charlotte, North Carolina. As of the end of fiscal 2024, we employed approximately 54,243 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 41 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- A downturn in global economic conditions could reduce demand for our products. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $8,391 million, a decrease of 2.9 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 19.9 percent and net income was
$1,123 million. The Medical Devices segment was our largest, contributing $3,342 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Medical Devices: $3,342 million
- Diagnostics: $3,294 million
- Pharmacy Services: $1,756 million

Results reflected the integration of prior acquisitions partly offset by
elevated wage inflation.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $3,698 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $598 million. Capital expenditures were $328 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $1.20 per share and authorised a $1,579 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
