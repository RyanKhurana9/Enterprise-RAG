# Evans Financial - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2024)

## Item 1. Business
Evans Financial is a financial company operating through three reportable segments: Payments,
Asset Management and Consumer Lending. Our principal executive offices are located in Portland, Oregon. As of the end of fiscal 2024, we employed approximately 33,774 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 24 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- Climate-related regulation and physical risks may increase our operating costs. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $34,287 million, an increase of 18.8 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 23.0 percent and net income was
$4,948 million. The Payments segment was our largest, contributing $13,645 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Payments: $13,645 million
- Asset Management: $11,028 million
- Consumer Lending: $9,615 million

Results reflected a favourable mix partly offset by
foreign-exchange headwinds.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $11,794 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $3,295 million. Capital expenditures were $1,397 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $1.64 per share and authorised a $2,001 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
