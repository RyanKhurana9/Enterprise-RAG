# Hale Technologies - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Hale Technologies is a software company operating through three reportable segments: Cloud Platform,
Enterprise Licences and Professional Services. Our principal executive offices are located in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 59,187 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 45 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Cybersecurity incidents could result in the loss of confidential data, regulatory penalties and reputational harm. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Acquisitions may not deliver the expected synergies and could divert management attention. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Our indebtedness could limit our flexibility in planning for or reacting to changes in our business. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances. Regulators in several jurisdictions have increased their scrutiny of this area, which could raise our compliance costs.
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. We monitor this exposure through our enterprise risk management framework and report it to the Audit Committee each quarter.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $27,977 million, an increase of 0.3 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 26.8 percent and net income was
$5,456 million. The Cloud Platform segment was our largest, contributing $11,660 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Cloud Platform: $11,660 million
- Enterprise Licences: $10,958 million
- Professional Services: $5,359 million

Results reflected cost discipline partly offset by
foreign-exchange headwinds.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $5,783 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $2,967 million. Capital expenditures were $1,808 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. The Board declared total dividends of $0.92 per share and authorised a $2,484 million share repurchase programme. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
