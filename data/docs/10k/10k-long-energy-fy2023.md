# Long Energy - Form 10-K Extract (Fiscal 2023)

## Item 1. Business
Long Energy is a energy company operating through three reportable segments: Upstream,
Refining and Renewables. Our principal executive offices are located in Atlanta, Georgia. As of the end of fiscal 2023, we employed approximately 58,539 people worldwide. We sell to customers in more
than 65 countries and our strategy emphasises disciplined capital
allocation, operational excellence and organic growth.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors
- We may be unable to attract and retain the highly skilled employees our strategy needs. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- We depend on third-party cloud providers; a prolonged outage could disrupt operations and damage customer relationships. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them.
- Supply-chain disruptions may delay product shipments and increase input costs. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations.
- Our international operations expose us to foreign-exchange volatility, sanctions regimes and differing tax laws. We have experienced such events in the past and expect to continue to face them. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Intense competition could reduce our market share or force us to lower prices. The occurrence of any of these events could have a material adverse effect on our business, financial condition and results of operations. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Litigation and regulatory investigations could result in significant fines or restrictions on our business. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.
- Changes in interest rates could adversely affect our net interest margin and the fair value of our fixed-income investment portfolio. Insurance coverage may not be sufficient to cover all losses arising from these risks. Although we maintain mitigation plans, there can be no assurance that these measures will be effective in all circumstances.

## Item 2. Properties
We own or lease manufacturing, distribution, research and office facilities in North America, Europe and Asia. We believe our facilities are suitable and adequate for our current needs and that additional space can be obtained on commercially reasonable terms if required.

## Item 3. Legal Proceedings
From time to time we are party to litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings arising in the ordinary course of business. While the outcome of these matters is inherently uncertain, management does not currently expect them to have a material adverse effect on our consolidated financial position.

## Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
We are exposed to market risks from changes in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates and commodity prices. We use derivative instruments, principally interest-rate swaps and foreign-currency forwards, to manage these exposures, and we do not enter into derivatives for speculative purposes. A hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material effect on our interest expense given the predominantly fixed-rate nature of our borrowings.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Total revenue for fiscal 2023 was $18,677 million, an increase of 9.4 percent compared with the prior year. Operating margin was 14.5 percent and net income was
$1,772 million. The Upstream segment was our largest, contributing $8,351 million of revenue.

Segment revenue:
- Upstream: $8,351 million
- Refining: $5,791 million
- Renewables: $4,534 million

Results reflected a favourable mix partly offset by
higher input costs.

## Liquidity and Capital Resources
Long-term debt totalled $13,887 million, and cash and cash equivalents were $2,433 million. Capital expenditures were $1,003 million, primarily for capacity expansion and technology infrastructure. We did not pay a dividend during the year and prioritised debt reduction. We believe
existing cash, operating cash flow and our revolving credit facility will be sufficient to
fund operations for at least the next twelve months.
